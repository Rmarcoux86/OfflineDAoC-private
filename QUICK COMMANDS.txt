OFFLINE DAOC - QUICK COMMANDS AND LAUNCHER SHORTCUTS

On the launcher, click + Lv.1 or + Lv.50 under a faction:
  Normal click: +1 bot
  Hold Shift while clicking: +10 bots
  Hold Ctrl while clicking: +100 bots

/tele X
  X = a gamebot's name. Teleports you to that bot.
  You can also right-click bot names on the launcher to teleport.

/mobs X
  X = a level. Shows names of mobs of that level.

/tele mob X
  X = the mob's exact name. Teleports you to a spawn.
  For dungeon mobs, the configured entrance approach is used where applicable.

/tc
  Teleports you to your realm's Realm Exchange NPC.

/stables
  Lists every stablemaster horse route in your realm, one page at a time,
  including the neutral dragonfly, gryphon and wyvern routes.
  /stables X shows page X.

/stables classic
  Jumps to your realm's Classic stable routes.

/stables si
  Jumps to your realm's Shrouded Isles stable routes.

/run new [seed]
  Expedition mode: starts a seeded roguelite run on a level-1 character with no
  realm points. Turns your Hardcore flag on (death ends the run and deletes the
  character) and takes you to act 1's camp. Same seed, same expedition.
/run status
/run journal
/run seed
/run abandon
  Show the act, level band, zone and objectives; open the journal; show the seed;
  give up the run (Hardcore flag off).
/run boons
  Show the boon offered after an act boss and the boons you have.
/run boon <1-3>
  Take one boon of the offer; it lasts the whole run.
/junk common|magic|rare
  Destroy Expedition items of that rarity and lower in your backpack for the
  merchant price (asks first; never Epic, Unique or non-Expedition items).
/junk auto off|common|magic
/junk status
  Sell such drops on the spot (saved per character); show the setting.

/fish
  Face open water with a fishing pole and type /fish to cast your line and relax.
  You keep casting after each catch until you move or type /fish stop.
  /fish active: you reel in each bite yourself by typing /fish within 4 seconds.
  /fish stats shows your fishing skill and best catch.
  /fish pole gives you a fishing pole if you don't have one.
  /fish spots lists fishing spots; /fish go <number> travels to one in your realm.
  /fish mark <name> saves the shore you stand on (facing water) as a new spot;
  /fish unmark <number> removes one you marked.

/grind
  Starts automated grinding with a companion bot group, including for AFK use.

/pull
  Orders your companion group and pets to engage your selected enemy. When possible,
  a tank makes first contact before the rest of the group joins the fight.

/train <line> <level>
  Trains a specialization to the chosen level using your available specialization
  points. Select a valid trainer for your class first.

/spawn
  Opens a menu of valid companion bots to summon.

/spawn X
  X = a class name, if you prefer not to use the menu or want a macro.

/raid 40
/raid 80
  Enables a 40- or 80-member companion raid. Use BEFORE /spawn.
  Requires level 50. The total includes your own character.

/aggressive
  Companions automatically attack what you attack and react defensively.

/defensive
  Companions engage nearby threats while staying near you, letting you pull mobs
  back to the group. Useful for harder content.

These controls apply to your companions, not autonomous gamebots.
/spawn 40 and /spawn 80 are not raid-size commands; use /raid 40 or /raid 80.
See docs/PLAY.md for setup and docs/LLM-QUICKSTART.md for modifying your own version.
